## What the GPL already allows

The GPL v3.0 permits commercial use. A company may run CircleSeeker, modify it,
and build services on it without asking anyone. What the GPL requires is that if
you *convey* the software or a derivative work - that is, distribute copies to
others - you do so under the GPL as well, with source. Running it yourself, or
offering a service over a network without handing out copies, is not conveying
and carries no such obligation (GPL v3.0, section 0).

## What the commercial license is for

It releases you from that copyleft obligation. Take it if you want to distribute
CircleSeeker, or a product containing it, without releasing your own source under
the GPL.
