## Changes affecting results

- Repeat candidates from the same read retain their own sequence, length and RCA
  support. Missing candidate metadata cannot borrow another repeat's metadata.
- Classification and deduplication preserve segment direction and circular
  traversal order. Existing CD-HIT clusters retain their baseline role; the
  preliminary patch's additional cluster partition and exact-consensus grouping
  are removed. Coordinate-only merging retains the original 10 bp tolerance
  while requiring equivalent directed cycles.
- A selected alignment spanning the full consensus cannot be combined with extra
  loci into a nonoverlapping chimeric partition. Inferred paths and cycles follow
  retained graph edges; segment strands survive curation and sequence extraction.
- Per-read RCA support is reconstructed from candidate support, separately from
  the molecule aggregate. This is RCA support, not genomic copy number.
- Cecc BEDPE contains N junctions for N segments, including the closing junction;
  endpoints respect strand. Consumers assuming N−1 junctions must adapt.
- LAST identity is measured from alignment strings. Unavailable LAST MAPQ,
  low-MAPQ and associated confidence values remain missing.
- Packaging rejects inconsistent confirmed Cecc sequence lengths; final FASTA
  excludes inferred candidates removed from the final catalogue.

Counts, IDs, directions, support values and benchmark metrics can change. The
endpoint numeric-comparison fix was already present in historical v1.5.1.

## Validation of the frozen repair source

- Linux/Python 3.12: 1,293 tests passed, one deselected; coverage 72.49%; mypy
  passed 59 source files. These are checks of `44c79b8`, distinct from new
  release-artifact installation checks and the older draft's CI.
- All 55 previously flagged real-data records passed targeted replay from saved
  original evidence. Two complete raw-input runs and the affected final inference
  stages were separately verified before continuing the full cohort.
- All 27 complete raw-input benchmarks passed output-integrity checks. Against
  original v1.5.1, Overall TP was 328,006 → 328,004 and FP 4,777 → 4,756.
  Equal-scenario mean F1 was 0.97145185 → 0.97145926. At the evaluator's four-decimal
  precision, 23 Overall F1 values were unchanged, three improved and one decreased.
- Three scenarios had a small decrease in at least one reported metric: human
  UMC rep3/10X and rep3/50X each lost one Cecc TP; rep3/30X gained one Uecc FP
  while its Overall FP fell by two. The author accepted small deviations and
  requested all computations finish with differences retained in the record.
- In the same 19 scenarios that exposed the rejected repair's +350 FP, the current
  source has unchanged TP (153,809) and three fewer FP (1,274 → 1,271) than v1.5.1.
- Fifteen of 33 real libraries completed: 58,405 → 58,401 calls. Nine retained
  identical total and U/M/C counts; six changed by one to three total calls.
  Length, per-read copy-sum and directed-closure checks passed. Real-library
  count changes cannot be labelled TP/FP without a per-molecule truth set.

Benchmark scoring follows the existing matching rules and does not independently
validate strand or circular order. Equal counts also do not imply identical rows
or sequences. The detailed repair contracts and replay evidence are documented
in [the development report](../development/cecc-integrity-fix.md).
